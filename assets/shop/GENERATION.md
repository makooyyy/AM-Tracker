# Иллюстрации магазина

Созданы встроенным инструментом image_gen. PNG-оригиналы сохранены в этой папке локально; оптимизированные WebP используются приложением. Прозрачность рамки сохранена.

## Небесная библиотека — celestial-library.webp

Create a premium illustrated wide landscape profile banner background for a manga reading tracker. A floating celestial library at midnight: luminous open book on right side, elegant crescent moon, drifting golden pages and tiny constellations, deep indigo and muted violet with antique gold accents. Painterly anime fantasy environment, sophisticated and restrained, detailed right third, dark quiet left half for white profile text and avatar overlay. No text, no letters, no logos, no UI, no borders. Landscape 3:2 composition.

## Лунный артефакт — celestial-frame.webp

Asset: transparent PNG avatar frame for a premium fantasy reading tracker. Single perfectly circular ornate celestial frame, centered, thin elegant antique-gold filigree with violet crystal crescent at top and a tiny open-book jewel at bottom. The central circular hole must be fully transparent and large (about 68 percent image diameter); all space outside frame fully transparent. Frame occupies 90 percent of square canvas, no portrait, no background, no checkerboard, no text. Delicate polished metal, luminous lilac accents, readable at 100px. Symmetric, refined, not bulky.
